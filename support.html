How can we help?

Goul Wallet is a non-custodial wallet for storing, sending, and receiving GU tokens. Below are answers to common questions. If you don't find what you need, reach out directly — we read every message.

Setting up a wallet

Open the app and choose Create Wallet to generate a new wallet, or Import Wallet if you already have a seed phrase. During setup, write down your recovery phrase and store it somewhere safe and offline — it's the only way to recover your wallet if you lose access to your device.

Backing up your wallet

Your recovery phrase is the only backup for your wallet. Goul Wallet never stores it on our servers, and we cannot recover it for you if it's lost. Keep a written copy in a secure location, and never share it with anyone.

Sending and receiving GU

From the home screen, tap Send to transfer tokens to another address, or Receive to view your wallet address and QR code for others to send to you. Double-check the recipient address before confirming any transfer — transactions on the blockchain cannot be reversed.

Deleting a wallet

You can remove a wallet from your device at any time. From the home screen, tap the portfolio card, then Manage Wallets → Delete Wallet, and confirm. To remove all wallets at once, go to Settings → Wallet Management → Delete All Wallets. Deleting a wallet permanently removes it and its keys from your device — this cannot be undone, so make sure you've backed up your recovery phrase first if you want to restore it later.

Face ID and security

Goul Wallet supports biometric authentication for an extra layer of protection. You can enable or disable it anytime in Settings → Security → Face ID. Your private keys are stored securely on your device and are never transmitted to us or any third party.
